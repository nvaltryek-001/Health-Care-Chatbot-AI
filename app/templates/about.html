About MedBot

MedBot is an open-source AI health-care chatbot. It helps people understand their symptoms, learn about common conditions and estimate heart-disease risk — and tells them clearly when to see a doctor.

How it works

1 · Safety checkEmergency and crisis phrases are detected first and answered with helpline numbers.
2 · Symptom extractionHundreds of symptom phrases are matched, with negation (“no fever”) and typo tolerance.
3 · Condition rankingAn IDF-weighted overlap score compares your symptoms with 30 condition profiles.
4 · Intent modelA TF-IDF + logistic-regression classifier handles greetings, help and other small talk.

The Heart Risk page uses a separate logistic-regression model trained on the UCI Cleveland heart-disease dataset. You can see its accuracy and the dataset on the Insights page.

Important limitations

  • MedBot is not a doctor and does not provide a diagnosis. It cannot examine you or run tests.
  • The knowledge base covers common conditions only. Rare or serious illnesses may present differently.
  • The heart model was trained on 302 patients from one hospital in the 1980s and may not generalise to you.
  • Never start, stop or change prescribed medicine based on this app.

In an emergency

Call 112 (India), 108 for an ambulance, or your local emergency number. For mental-health support in India, call Tele-MANAS 14416 (free, 24×7).

Dataset

Heart-disease data: UCI Machine Learning Repository, Heart Disease (Cleveland), CC BY 4.0.
